Step 06 · Prompt12 / 25

Sign in first (a free account works), then paste the notes. Two jobs here. First, the Enhance prompt button (click the + at the bottom-left of the box): it adds details about spacing, fonts and responsiveness. Save the enhanced version, that's your master prompt for every tool that follows. Second, press send: Bolt builds a design of its own from that prompt. Also: Bolt needs a free account: sign in first, paste the spec, tap + and then "Enhance prompt".

Step 11 · Ship, the finishing touch21 / 25

Prices vary by name and ending, and the renewal price can differ from the first-year price, so check both. .com is not always the cheapest: endings like .site, .xyz, .party or .science are often cheaper and more fun. To connect the domain, just ask Claude: it can do it for you or walk you through each record. DNS changes can take a little while to kick in.

Also: Where to buy: Cloudflare or GoDaddy. "Enforce HTTPS" is the padlock switch in GitHub Pages settings.

Full list:

  • Search for a name at Cloudflare or GoDaddy
  • .com isn't always the cheapest: a fun ending like .site, .xyz, .party or .science can cost less
  • Check the first-year price and the renewal price
  • Ask Claude to connect the domain for you, or to walk you through it
  • Turn on 'Enforce HTTPS' once it's ready
